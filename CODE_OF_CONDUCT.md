# Code of Conduct

Watchtrail is a friendly, hobby-scale project. We want it to stay that way.

## Our standard

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith.
- Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give feedback that is specific and useful, and accept it the same way.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks, in any language.
- Keep discussion on topic: the software and how to make it better.
- Do not post other people's private information.

## Piracy and content

Watchtrail tracks what *you* watch from servers *you* have access to. Do not use issues, pull requests or discussions to share, request or advertise copyrighted media, private server credentials or access links.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This text is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
